# Phase 4 — Distribution and Growth

## Context For New Models

This phase begins after Gate 2 (5+ paying subscribers with positive retention). The founder has a working product with validated signal quality and initial paying customers. This phase focuses on growing to 75 subscribers and £3,000+ MRR by month 12. The technical product largely exists — the challenge is distribution.

**The core problem this phase solves:** Getting from 5 subscribers to 75 subscribers without a large existing audience.

---

## The Distribution Reality

The founder is a data scientist not a marketer. The biggest risk to this business is building a technically excellent product that nobody finds. Distribution requires consistent effort across channels that do not feel natural to most technical founders.

**The honest time allocation:**
- 40% of non-job time: Content creation and community
- 35% of non-job time: Product improvement based on subscriber feedback
- 25% of non-job time: Creator partnerships and outreach

If this split feels wrong — it is the right split. The product largely exists. Distribution does not.

## Channel 1 — Substack (Most Important)

**Start during Phase 2. Not Phase 4.**

By the time Phase 4 begins, the Substack should already have 200+ subscribers from Phase 2 content. This is your primary launch audience.

### Content Strategy

**Publication positioning:**
Data-driven prediction market intelligence. Not picks. Not tips. Rigorous analysis with methodology shown.

**Weekly content cadence:**

```
Monday: Weekly wallet intelligence briefing
"What the smart money did last week on Polymarket"
- Notable positions opened by top wallets
- Markets with significant smart money activity
- Anonymised wallet analysis (not addresses until paid tier)
- Outcome tracking: how did last week's signals resolve?

Wednesday: Market analysis (alternating themes)
Week A: Deep dive on one interesting market
Week B: Cross-market consistency gaps detected
Week C: Model signal analysis — what's getting GREEN/RED this week

Friday: The honest track record update (monthly)
- Signal accuracy for the month
- Coverage verification (empirical vs 90% target)
- Notable misses and why they happened
- Subscriber count and growth
```

**Tone:** Analytical, honest, methodology-forward. Never "buy this." Always "our analysis shows" and "here's what the data suggests."

### Substack SEO and Discovery

Apply to Substack's recommendation network. Other prediction market and finance Substack writers can recommend your publication to their subscribers. A single recommendation from a writer with 10,000 subscribers can add hundreds of subscribers overnight.

Target publications to seek recommendations from:
- Cheap Convexity (Jon Turek) — macro and prediction markets
- Next Event Horizon — prediction market analysis
- Any political analysis newsletter using Polymarket data

**Polymarket Substack partnership:**
Polymarket has an active partnership with Substack and sponsors creator cohorts.
Apply here: https://polymarket.com/oracle (their own newsletter)
Being in their sponsored cohort gives distribution from Polymarket's own audience.

## Channel 2 — Twitter/X

**Target:** 1,000 engaged followers in the prediction market space by month 12.

### Daily Posting Strategy

```
High engagement content (post 3x per week):
- Notable wallet positions opened (anonymised)
- Interesting consistency gaps in real time
- Market scoring updates: "Our model just flagged [market] as GREEN"
- Honest outcome tracking: "We called [market] GREEN last week. It resolved [outcome]."

Educational content (post 2x per week):
- Methodology explanations (how do we score wallets?)
- Conformal prediction explainers
- Prediction market mechanics for new traders

Community content (post daily):
- Engage with @Polymarket, @KalshiHQ posts
- Reply to prediction market discussion with data
- Share interesting market movements with context
```

### Growth tactics:

Do not buy followers. Do not spam. Build genuine reputation first.

Engage specifically with:
- Prediction market Twitter accounts with 1,000-50,000 followers
- Political analysts who reference Polymarket data
- Economics and macro accounts that use prediction markets
- Journalists who cite prediction market odds

## Channel 3 — Polymarket Discord

**Goal:** Become a trusted, helpful presence before any product promotion.

**Timeline:** 2-3 months of genuine participation before any mention of the product.

**Approach:**
- Answer questions about prediction market mechanics
- Share analysis when relevant to ongoing discussions
- Help new traders understand how markets work
- Never spam or promote unprompted

**When to mention the product:**
Only when directly relevant to a conversation. "We actually track this type of thing — here's what our wallet analysis shows." Not as a pitch — as a genuine contribution.

## Channel 4 — Creator Partnerships

**When to approach:** After having 20+ paying subscribers and 3+ months of published track record.

**Do not approach creators before having:**
- Working product
- Paying subscribers
- Published honest track record (including bad periods)
- Clear revenue share terms

### Target Creator Tiers

**Tier 1 — Prediction market focused Substack writers (highest conversion, smallest audience)**

Target publications:
- Next Event Horizon — prediction market analysis newsletter
- The Closing Line — prediction market monitoring
- Jon Turek's Cheap Convexity — macro + prediction markets

Why they convert: Their audience is exactly your target customer. Even 5,000 subscribers at 5% conversion = 250 potential subscribers.

**Tier 2 — Larger newsletters using Polymarket data (lower conversion, larger audience)**

Target publications:
- Political analysis newsletters with 10,000-100,000 subscribers
- Economics/macro writers who reference prediction markets
- Any Substack in Polymarket's sponsored creator cohort

Why they convert: Large enough that even 0.5% conversion is meaningful.

**Tier 3 — Twitter/X accounts (unpredictable, high ceiling)**

Target accounts:
- Prediction market analysts with 10,000-100,000 followers
- Crypto/finance accounts that post about Polymarket
- Political forecasting accounts

### Partnership Terms

```
Structure: Revenue share on referred subscribers
Rate: 35% of monthly subscription revenue for lifetime of referred subscriber
Tracking: Unique Stripe referral links per creator
Payment: Monthly via Stripe payouts or manual bank transfer

Example:
Creator refers 30 Pro subscribers (£45/month each)
Creator earns: 30 × £45 × 35% = £472.50/month ongoing
Founder earns: 30 × £45 × 65% = £877.50/month from their referrals

No minimum commitment
No exclusivity required
Creator keeps their editorial independence — they decide what to say
```

## Product Improvements in Phase 4

Based on subscriber feedback — build what paying customers ask for, not what seems cool.

**Likely requests to anticipate:**

```
1. Better mobile experience
   Current Streamlit app is not mobile-optimised
   Solution: Mobile-responsive CSS in Streamlit or simple PWA wrapper

2. Historical signal performance by category
   "Show me how well the signals work on geopolitical markets specifically"
   Solution: Add category filter to performance dashboard

3. Watchlist functionality
   "Alert me only on markets in my watchlist"
   Solution: Per-subscriber market watchlist in PostgreSQL

4. Export to CSV
   "I want to analyse your market scores in my own spreadsheet"
   Solution: Simple CSV download button on market feed

5. Kalshi integration
   "Show me Kalshi markets too"
   Solution: Add Kalshi API alongside Polymarket (no wallet intelligence on Kalshi)
```

**Do not build in Phase 4 without subscriber requests:**
- Automated trading execution (regulatory risk)
- Social/community features (maintenance overhead)
- Native mobile app (premature optimisation)
- News sentiment layer (save for Phase 5)

## Monthly KPIs to Track

```
Revenue metrics:
- MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)
- MRR growth rate (target: 15-20% month-over-month)
- Churn rate (target: below 5% monthly)
- Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
- LTV:CAC ratio (target: above 10:1)

Product metrics:
- Daily active users on dashboard
- Alert engagement rate (% of alerts clicked)
- Feature usage by tier

Signal quality metrics:
- Wallet intelligence accuracy (published monthly)
- Conformal coverage verification (published monthly)
- Detection lag (target: under 60 seconds)
- Signal volume (number of actionable alerts per week)

Distribution metrics:
- Substack subscriber count
- Twitter followers
- Source of new subscribers (Substack, Discord, referral, organic)
```
