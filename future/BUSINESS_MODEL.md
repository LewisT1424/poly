## What The Business Is

A data intelligence platform for serious Polymarket traders combining on-chain wallet tracking, ML market scoring, and smart money alerts.

**What it is:**
- Financial data intelligence and research platform
- Subscription SaaS product
- Built on publicly available blockchain data

**What it is not:**
- A financial adviser or investment service
- A gambling operator
- A trading bot or automated execution service

---

## Value Proposition

**One sentence:** The only prediction market intelligence platform combining rigorous on-chain wallet scoring with machine learning signals and mathematically guaranteed confidence intervals.

**For the customer:** "We help you find the 5 markets worth your attention today, alert you when smart money moves, and flag arbitrage opportunities — so you can trade on your knowledge more effectively."

## Customer Segments

### Primary — Serious Informed Retail Traders
- Deploys £500-5,000 per trade regularly
- Has domain expertise (politics, economics, geopolitics)
- Researches manually today — your tool replaces hours of work
- No ability or desire to build their own data pipeline
- Active year-round not just during elections

### Secondary — New Serious Traders
People transitioning from casual to serious (Month 2-3 on Polymarket). Continuously replenishing segment. Free tier catches them at discovery.

### Not Your Customer
- Top quant traders (build their own)
- Casual users under £200 total deposit (won't pay)
- UK-based traders who cannot access Polymarket

## Open Methodology Commitment

**Publish openly (builds trust):**
- Four dimensions of wallet scoring in plain English
- Why conformal prediction provides mathematical guarantees
- Monthly signal performance including bad months
- Which market categories show interesting activity

**Never publish:**
- Specific top wallet addresses
- Scoring formula weights and thresholds
- Alert detection logic
- Feature engineering implementation
- Model code or weights

## Legal Framework

### What You Are
Financial data intelligence and research platform. Not FCA regulated. Not UKGC regulated.

### Before First Payment
```
Terms of Service: £300-500 (lawyer, not a template)
  Key clauses: research not advice, not FCA/UKGC regulated,
  past performance disclaimer, liability limitation

Privacy Policy: £100-200 (GDPR compliant)

UK Ltd: £12 via Companies House
```

### UK/Polymarket Legal Position
Polymarket is geoblocked in UK. Building analytics on public blockchain data is legally distinct from operating a gambling service. Market primarily to US and international traders. Do not explicitly market to UK residents for trading on Polymarket.

### Language Rules
```
Never: "Buy this market" or "Sell this position"
Always: "Our model indicates X" or "Wallet intelligence suggests Y"
```

## Decision Gates

```
Gate 1 (Month 3) — Paper Trading Validation
Pass: 62%+ wallet accuracy, 50+ signals, positive P&L
Fail: Below 55%, fewer than 20 signals

Gate 2 (Month 5) — Product Market Fit
Pass: 5+ paying subscribers, positive retention
Fail: No paying subscribers after 90 days

Gate 3 (Month 12) — Scale Decision
Pass: 50+ subscribers, £2,000+ MRR, growing
→ Apply pre-seed, consider full time
Fail: Below 30 subscribers, high churn
→ Honest assessment — park or pivot
```
